Add `inlang cloud`. It lists what is coming in inlang Cloud: automatic handoff between designers, developers and translators; AI translation that follows your terminology and tone and fits the design; and smart message keys. It then opens a short form where you can tell us what your team needs. For Paraglide JS projects, Paraglide JS is pre-selected in the form. `--no-open` prints the form's URL instead of opening it; the browser is never opened in CI or when the output is piped. `--json` prints the features and the form's URL as JSON. `inlang --help` points to the command.
